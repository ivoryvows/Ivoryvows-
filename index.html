About Ivory Vows

Created for your once-in-a-lifetime moments.

Ivory Vows creates elegant digital invitations for weddings, birthdays, baby showers, festivals and every celebration that deserves to be remembered.

We believe an invitation is more than information. It is the first feeling your guests receive before your celebration even begins.

That's why every design is created with attention to typography, movement, colour, storytelling and the little details that make your event feel like yours.

Choose a design from our collection, share your details, tell us what you would like customized, and we will create your invitation with care.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know before ordering.

What types of invitations do you create? +
We create wedding websites, wedding video invitations, birthday video invitations, baby shower invitations, festival invitations and custom invitations for other occasions.
Can I customize a template? +
Yes. Once you choose a template, you can send us your event details and customization requirements.
How long does it take? +
Your invitation is generally delivered within 2 days after payment confirmation and after we receive all required information and customization details.
How do I order? +
Choose your favourite template and send us a DM on Instagram. We will guide you through payment and collect the details needed for your invitation.
Can I see the price first? +
Yes. Send us your country and the invitation template you are interested in and we will provide the applicable price.
