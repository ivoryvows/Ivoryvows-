ABOUT IVORY VOWS

Created For Your Most Beautiful Moments

Ivory Vows is a digital invitation studio focused on creating elegant, thoughtful and memorable invitations for life's special occasions. We started with a simple idea: your invitation should feel just as special as the event itself. Instead of a standard invitation that simply gives your guests the date and venue, we love creating something that sets the mood and gives them a little feeling of what is to come.


Our designs are created with attention to typography, movement, colours, details and the overall experience. Whether you prefer something minimal and elegant, romantic and floral, luxurious and royal, or cute and playful, we want your invitation to feel like it belongs to your celebration.


Most importantly, we believe in keeping the ordering process simple and personal. You choose the design, send us your details, and we take care of turning those details into a beautiful digital invitation.

OUR PROCESS

Simple From Start To Finish

  1. Browse our invitation collections.
  2. Choose the design you love.
  3. Click Customize and enter your country.
  4. Copy the prepared message and open Instagram.
  5. Send the message to @ivoryvows_invites.
  6. We will send you the pricing and payment details for your country and selected design.
  7. Confirm your payment.
  8. Send us all the information required for your invitation.
  9. We create your invitation and deliver it within 2 days after payment confirmation and receipt of all required details.
